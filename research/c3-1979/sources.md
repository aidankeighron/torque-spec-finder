# C3 1979 — Source Inventory

Read this before using any value in the sibling files. Two sources agreeing means nothing if
they are the same document wearing different hats.

| Appears at | URL |

| Vette Registry / C3VR "1965–1982 Torque Specs" | https://www.vetteregistry.net/C3-corvette-tips-tricks/brakes-steering-suspension/7/19651982-torque-specs/45 |

The CorvetteForum poster states the table came from "VANSTEEL parts catalogue (page 9)", and the
other copies are numerically identical. **Treat as a single tier-D/C vendor source.** Its
cross-appearances must never be counted as corroboration.

Additional caution: Van Steel's own page silently interleaves OE specs with **Van Steel / QA1 /
Wilwood aftermarket** hardware, and lists part-number-keyed rows (`FS-37`, `FS-38K`, `SR-17K`)
whose values apply to *their* parts, not the factory joint.

### Lineage C — Chilton, Corvette 1963–1983  ⭐ strongest single artifact

- **Repair & Tune-Up Guide, Corvette 1963 to 1983** (Chilton), 346 pp, full text
  https://www.corvettebergen.com/ownersmanual/c3_repair.pdf

Read directly (not via snippets) by the research agents. The only source here that gives
multi-step **procedures** — bearing adjustment sequences, "lower the car then torque",
single-use spindle nut, criss-cross pan pattern — rather than bare numbers.

**But:** it is third-party, its PDF text extraction destroys table column alignment on the
engine spec page, and on at least two safety-critical fasteners (upper ball joint nut, rear
camber cam nut) it contradicts both itself and GM. Treat as **tier C**, not A.

## The one source worth acquiring

**The 1979 GM Corvette Shop Manual.** It is the only thing that resolves the ball joint, camber
cam, caliper bridge bolt, and differential cover disagreements. Everything in this archive is a
proxy for it. Options noted in the C5 research: reprints from Helm/Zip/Mid America, or ACDelco
TDS — though TDS coverage starts at 1998, so it will **not** help here. A paper or PDF reprint
of the 1979 manual is the path.
