**Chilton procedure note (Corvette-specific, lineage C):**
> "Dip all main bearing cap bolts in clean oil, and torque all main bearing caps, excluding the
> thrust bearing cap, to specifications… Tighten the thrust bearing bolts finger tight. To align
> the thrust bearing, pry the crankshaft the extent of its axial travel several times, holding the
> last movement toward the front of the engine… Torque the thrust bearing cap to specifications."
> "NOTE: The thrust bearing must be aligned before torquing cap bolts."

### Pinion nut — IRRECONCILABLE: value vs. method

| Approach | Value | Source | Lineage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Absolute torque** | **200–220 lb-ft** | 1980 Corvette GM Shop Manual | CF 753043 | G |
| **Rotating preload** | "Tighten nut to remove end play — continue alternately tightening in small increments and checking preload with torque wrench until torque required to rotate pinion is…" **20–30 lb-in** (new bearings & seal) / **5–15 lb-in** (used parts reinstalled) | **1973 Chevrolet Overhaul Manual** | CAC 23034 | G |
| Pinion bearing preload | 14–19 lb-in | Yukon installation PDF | CF 753043 | M |

**These do not reconcile.** One is a torque target; the other explicitly says tighten until
preload is reached. A flat 200–220 lb-ft **can crush new bearings**. Preload governs.

## Procedural notes to carry into the schema

1. **Dry vs lubricated threads.** Summit: "stock-type bolts with light engine oil applied to the
   threads and the underside of the bolt head. Moly and other lubes offer reduced friction and
   increased bolt tension, which will affect the torque figure." Brakes (CF 4933379): "Torque
   values are for dry threads so if you put any lubricant or never seize on the threads you need
   to reduce the torque value." AJK flags per-fastener: intake = non-hardening sealant,
   exhaust = anti-seize, water pump = sealant, cam sprocket & flywheel = thread locker,
   spark plugs = no lubricant or sealer.

2. **Retorque warning, driveline.** Chilton: "The carrier support bracket bolts frequently work
   loose, causing vibration and rear axle hop. Periodic torquing of these four bolts will
   eliminate this problem." This is a maintenance note, not a spec — needs its own field.
